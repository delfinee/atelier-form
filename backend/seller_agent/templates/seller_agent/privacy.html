Information used

We use your account email and shop permissions, a one-way hash of your WeChat app-specific identifier, products, inventory, order and delivery details, messages and confirmed change records to provide seller operations and an audit history. Devices and service providers

The website uses essential session and CSRF cookies. The Mini Program stores a temporary login token, your privacy choice, current conversation identifier and pending request details. WeChat provides authentication; the hosting provider processes service data. {% if seller_ai_enabled %}DeepSeek processes your messages, limited recent conversation, product names, prices, stock and options, and order identifiers, payment/dispatch status and line quantities to understand natural-language requests. Model output is validated and every business change requires your confirmation.{% else %}AI is disabled on this deployment; guided commands remain available.{% endif %} We do not sell personal information or add advertising trackers.

Retention and requests

Mini Program login tokens expire after eight hours; linking requests expire after five minutes. Business and confirmation records remain in the seller database. Signing out clears local sign-in data but does not delete business records or unlink an account. You can unlink WeChat in the website's WeChat connection section. Contact the operator for access, correction, deletion or account closure. Some transaction and audit records may need to be retained where applicable obligations require them.

Product photos and clipboard

Photos you choose to upload in the website editor are processed and saved with the product. The native Mini Program does not request camera, photo library, location, contacts or microphone access. Its copy buttons write selected links or the support email to the clipboard; it does not read your clipboard.
