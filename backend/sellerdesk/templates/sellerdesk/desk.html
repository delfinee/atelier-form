HOW THE DEMO WORKS

Two sides. One order.

  1. Customer sends a message

    Open a customer preview and ask about a product.

  2. Seller reviews and quotes

    Reply, select products and send a quote from the inbox.

  3. Customer confirms and pays

    Review the quote and simulate payment in the preview.

  4. Seller packs and ships

    Complete the packing check, then enter carrier and tracking details.

Each preview is linked to one conversation in your workspace. You can share its link with a teammate to test the customer side.
